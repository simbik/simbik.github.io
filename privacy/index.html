Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Who this policy covers

Lev Verbitskii, publishing as Simbik, is responsible for the practices described here. This policy currently applies to Rust Outpost (com.simbik.rustoutpost) and Bloomhouse: Cozy Garden (com.simbik.bloomhouse; in Russian, «Цветущий дом: уютный сад»). Other Simbik games and apps are covered only when explicitly listed here.

Data in the games

Rust Outpost does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer or third parties. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, online chat, or in-app purchases. The game does not sell user data or use it for tracking.

Bloomhouse does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer or third parties. It does not require an account or sign-in and does not include advertising, analytics, or online chat. It may offer optional in-app purchases, such as packs of golden leaves and a garden club subscription; these are processed by Apple, and Simbik does not receive your payment details. The game does not sell user data or use it for tracking.

Local progress and settings

Each game stores progress and preferences on your device to provide gameplay and remember your settings. These files are not sent to Simbik. You can remove local data using your device’s app-data controls or by removing the game and its saved files. Device or operating-system backups may retain copies under your platform’s settings and policies.

If you contact support

If you email lev.verbitskii@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a problem description, device details, or screenshots. We use this information only to respond and resolve your request, based on our legitimate interest in providing support. Our email provider processes these messages to deliver and store correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. We do not sell support information or share it for advertising; disclosure may occur where required by law.

Stores and this website

Google Play and the Apple App Store process purchases and operate their platforms under their own privacy policies. Simbik does not receive your payment card details. This website uses no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

The games listed here do not collect personal data from players, including children. Parents can control in-app purchases in Bloomhouse with Apple’s Screen Time and Ask to Buy settings. If a child has sent personal information through support, a parent or guardian may contact us to request deletion.

Your choices and rights

For personal information you provide through support, you may request access, correction, or deletion, or object to or request restriction of processing. Other rights, including portability where applicable and the right to complain to a data protection authority, depend on applicable law. Contact lev.verbitskii@gmail.com to make a request.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if these practices change. Questions about privacy can be sent to Lev Verbitskii (Simbik) at lev.verbitskii@gmail.com.
